M5Dial Music

Bluetooth media remote for iPhone, Android and Windows, made for the steering wheel. It also shows the title, artist, progress, time and notifications (iPhone natively, Android with Gadgetbridge, Windows with a small PC app). It drives Govee H7090 LEDs too.

1. Install the firmware

One firmware per platform, so the M5Dial only runs what you use. Plug the M5Dial in over USB-C, pick your platform, click the button and pick its serial port.

No port? Unplug the M5Dial, hold the StampS3 BOOT (G0) button, then plug it back in.

2. Pair your device

  • iPhone: Settings → Bluetooth → M5Dial Music, then accept pairing and "Share System Notifications". Controls, track info, time, calls and notifications.
  • Android (Android firmware): Settings → Connected devices → Pair new device → Bangle.js M5Dial (Gadgetbridge only recognises watch names). That gives the controls. For track info, time, calls and notifications, install Gadgetbridge (free, F-Droid), tap + and add Bangle.js M5Dial, then allow notification access.
  • Windows (Windows firmware): Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device → Bluetooth → M5Dial Music. That gives the controls. For track info, time and notifications, run the PC app (Python): pip install -r requirements.txt, then pythonw m5dial_pc.py.

Android and Windows have no built-in way to share track info over Bluetooth: without Gadgetbridge or the PC app, the screen shows "Connected" and the controls still work.

3. Controls

  • Turn the wheel: volume − / +
  • Press the wheel or tap the screen: play / pause
  • Hold the wheel 2 s: power off (on battery)
  • No input for 20 s dims the screen; no device for 10 min powers off (WAKE button to turn back on). Both delays are adjustable.
  • Swipe left / right: next / previous track
  • iPhone: tap the icons at the bottom = shuffle / repeat, tap elsewhere = play / pause
  • Incoming call (iPhone, Android with Gadgetbridge): press = answer, hold = decline
  • Notification: app name and sender (contact in messaging apps), message text optional · press / tap / swipe = close
  • Swipe up or down: Music, LED, Stopwatch, Clock, Sensors, System and Settings pages
  • Stopwatch: press = start / pause, swipe left or right = reset
  • Clock: time, date and cabin temperature (with a sensor unit)
  • Sensors: temperature, humidity, pressure and altitude from the M5Stack ENV III and IMU Pro Mini units (Port A or B, found at power on)
  • Settings: turn = choose, press = edit then done: IMU direction, wheel, delays, day / night / auto brightness from the iPhone time, notifications (off / sender / full), accent colour, paired devices

4. Govee H7090 LEDs

  • On the LED page, the M5Dial finds and connects to the H7090 bar by itself.
  • Wheel: brightness · Press: on / off · Swipe left / right: colour · Short hold (0.6 s): the wheel switches to hue mode (colour wheel)
  • Bottom line: Connected in green or Disconnected in red

Close the Govee Home app on your phone: the bar only accepts one connection at a time.

5. Steering wheel mount

Made for the OMP Corsica wheel, ref. OD0-1954 (suede, black / blue spokes, Ø 350 mm, 95 mm dish): ompracing.com. The M5Dial sits between the bottom and right spokes, one M5 screw in each spoke's Ø 16 hole nearest the hub.

Print (PETG or ASA, 4 perimeters, 30–40 % infill):

  • support_volant.stl ×1, flat underside, supports from the bed under the M5Dial ring only
  • coque_batterie.stl ×1 (battery shell), floor down, no supports
  • No sensor units: print support_volant_sans_capteurs.stl and coque_batterie_sans_capteurs.stl instead (no ENV III slot, no IMU Pro Mini holder)
  • couvercle_batterie.stl ×1 (shell lid), lay it on its flat plate
  • cache_ecrou.stl ×2 (low nut caps), flat base
  • cache_factice.stl ×1 (dummy cap that clips into the left spoke's hole), peg on the bed, supports under the dome rim
  • Optional fit check first: support_volant_test.stl (the two pads only)

Hardware: LiPo 103450 (50 × 34 × 10 mm); per spoke (bottom and right) an M5 × 14 hex screw, an M5 × 20 fender washer (a plain washer drops into the hole) and an M5 nut. Optional: M5Stack IMU Pro Mini (Port A, holder on the battery shell, inside the tube) and ENV III (Port B, slot on the outside of the tube) with short Grove cables.

Assembly:

  1. M5Dial into the ring from the top, clamp ring screwed on from underneath through the tube windows.
  2. Battery plugged in (check the polarity), slid into the shell, lead up through the slot, lid clipped on.
  3. Shell collar into the tube from underneath, then a 35° turn clockwise (seen from below) until it clicks.
  4. Mount behind the two spokes, screw + washer + nut per spoke, nut caps pressed on, driver side; dummy cap clipped into the left spoke.
  5. Turn the wheel lock to lock: nothing must touch the stalks.

Print files also coming on MakerWorld. Other wheels: Fusion 360 scripts with editable dimensions in hardware/fusion.
