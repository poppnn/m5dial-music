M5Dial Music

Bluetooth media remote for iPhone, Android and Windows. On iPhone it also shows the title, artist, progress, calls and notifications.

1. Install the firmware

Plug the M5Dial in over USB-C, click the button and pick its serial port.

Firmware version: …

Your browser does not support Web Serial: use Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over HTTPS.

No port? Unplug the M5Dial, hold the StampS3 BOOT (G0) button, then plug it back in.

2. Pair your device

  • iPhone: Settings → Bluetooth → M5Dial Music, then accept pairing and "Share System Notifications". Controls, track info, time, calls and notifications.
  • Android: Settings → Connected devices → Pair new device → M5Dial Music. Controls only.
  • Windows: Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device → Bluetooth → M5Dial Music. Controls only.

Android and Windows do not share track info over Bluetooth: the screen then shows "Connected".

3. Controls

  • Turn the wheel: volume − / +
  • Press the wheel or tap the screen: play / pause
  • Hold the wheel 2 s: power off (on battery)
  • No input for 20 s dims the screen; no device for 10 min powers off (WAKE button to turn back on). Both delays are adjustable.
  • Swipe left / right: next / previous track
  • iPhone: tap the icons at the bottom = shuffle / repeat, double tap = "Like"
  • Incoming call (iPhone): press = answer, hold = decline · notification: app name and count, press / tap / swipe = close
  • Swipe up or down: Music, LED, Stopwatch, Clock, System and Settings pages
  • Stopwatch: press = start / pause, swipe left or right = reset
  • Settings: turn = choose, press = edit then done (IMU direction, wheel, delays, day / night / auto brightness from the iPhone time, paired devices)

4. Govee H7090 LEDs

  • On the LED page, the M5Dial finds and connects to the H7090 bar by itself.
  • Wheel: brightness · Press: on / off · Swipe left / right: colour · Short hold (0.6 s): the wheel switches to hue mode (colour wheel)

Close the Govee Home app on your phone: the bar only accepts one connection at a time.
