M5Dial Music

Bluetooth media remote for iPhone, Android and Windows, made for the steering wheel. It also shows the title, artist, progress, time and notifications (iPhone natively, Android with Gadgetbridge, Windows with a small PC app). It drives Govee H7090 LEDs too.

1. Install the firmware

One firmware per platform, so the M5Dial only runs what you use. Plug the M5Dial in over USB-C, pick your platform, click the button and pick its serial port.

No port? Unplug the M5Dial, hold the StampS3 BOOT (G0) button, then plug it back in.

2. Pair your device

  • iPhone: Settings → Bluetooth → M5Dial Music, then accept pairing and "Share System Notifications". Controls, track info, time, calls and notifications.
  • Android (Android firmware): Settings → Connected devices → Pair new device → Bangle.js M5Dial (Gadgetbridge only recognises watch names). That gives the controls. For track info, time, calls and notifications, install Gadgetbridge (free, F-Droid), tap + and add Bangle.js M5Dial, then allow notification access.
  • Windows (Windows firmware): Settings → Bluetooth & devices → Add device → Bluetooth → M5Dial Music. That gives the controls. For track info, time and notifications, run the PC app (Python): pip install -r requirements.txt, then pythonw m5dial_pc.py.

Adding a second phone or PC: once a device is paired, a new one is only accepted during the first 2 minutes after the M5Dial is switched on (so nobody nearby can pair with it). Switch it off and on, then pair.

Android and Windows have no built-in way to share track info over Bluetooth: without Gadgetbridge or the PC app, the screen shows "Connected" and the controls still work.

3. Controls

  • Turn the wheel: volume − / +
  • Press the wheel or tap the screen: play / pause
  • Hold the wheel 2 s: power off (on battery)
  • No input for 20 s dims the screen; no device for 10 min powers off (WAKE button to turn back on). Both delays are adjustable.
  • Swipe left / right: next / previous track
  • iPhone: tap the icons at the bottom = shuffle / repeat, tap elsewhere = play / pause
  • Incoming call (iPhone, Android with Gadgetbridge): tap the green button = answer, the red one = decline (or press / hold the wheel)
  • Notification: app name and sender (contact in messaging apps), message text optional · press / tap / swipe = close
  • Swipe up or down: Music, LED, Stopwatch, Clock, System and Settings pages (order set in the Web UI)
  • No phone connected: press, tap or swipe the waiting screen to open the pages anyway (LEDs, stopwatch...)
  • Stopwatch: press = start / pause, swipe left or right = reset
  • Clock: time and date
  • Settings: turn = choose, press = edit then done: wheel, delays, day / night / auto brightness from the iPhone time, notifications (off / sender / full), accent colour, paired devices

4. Govee H7090 LEDs

  • On the LED page, the M5Dial finds and connects to the H7090 bar by itself.
  • Wheel: brightness · Press: on / off · Swipe left / right: colour · Short hold (0.6 s): the wheel switches to hue (colour wheel), and back
  • Bottom line: Connected in green or Disconnected in red

Close the Govee Home app on your phone: the bar only accepts one connection at a time.

5. Steering wheel mount

Made for the OMP Corsica wheel, ref. OD0-1954 (suede, black / blue spokes, Ø 350 mm, 95 mm dish): ompracing.com. The M5Dial sits between the bottom and right spokes, one M5 screw in each spoke's Ø 16 hole nearest the hub.

Print (PETG or ASA, 4 perimeters, 30–40 % infill):

  • support_volant.stl ×1, flat underside, supports from the bed under the M5Dial ring only
  • coque_batterie.stl ×1 (LiPo battery shell), floor down, no supports; with it print support_volant_lipo.stl instead of support_volant.stl (bayonet at the tube foot)
  • couvercle_batterie.stl ×1 (shell lid), lay it on its flat plate
  • Or removable 16350 battery (instead of the shell and lid): support_cartouche_16350.stl ×1 (holder, rises inside the tube: it hangs only 17 mm below the mount), floor down; per battery cartouche_16350.stl bottom down, and bouchon_cartouche_16350.stl. Connection on top of the cell with 2 Dupont jumper ends: female ones clipped into the cartridge cradle, male ones clipped into the holder (wires straight up to the M5Dial); the jumper on the engraved “+” side is +; both cell wires come up a long open slot and the sliding cap hides them. A pen spring in the holder's back hole pushes the cartridge out when you pinch the two arm ends.
  • Charging dock (optional): chargeur_cartouche_16350.stl base down and couvercle_chargeur_16350.stl upside down; the charge board of a JNR puff (marked SIC C023-M22 V1.5) in the bay, display up and visible through the lid, its USB-C socket flush with the front (red + / black - wires to 2 male jumper ends, + on the “+” side, a spring in its back hole). Unsolder the puff sensor first so it can never heat.
  • cache_ecrou.stl ×2 (low nut caps), flat base
  • cache_factice.stl ×1 (dummy cap that clips into the left spoke's hole), peg on the bed, supports under the dome rim
  • Optional fit check first: support_volant_test.stl (the two pads only)

Hardware: LiPo 103450 (50 × 34 × 10 mm); per spoke (bottom and right) an M5 × 14 hex screw, an M5 × 20 fender washer (a plain washer drops into the hole) and an M5 nut. Or for the removable cartridge: a 16350 850 mAh cell per cartridge (from a JNR disposable puff) and 4 Dupont jumper ends (2 female, 2 male).

Assembly:

  1. M5Dial into the ring from the top, clamp ring screwed on from underneath through the tube windows.
  2. Battery plugged in (check the polarity), slid into the shell, lead up through the slot, lid clipped on.
  3. Shell collar into the tube from underneath, then a 35° turn clockwise (seen from below) until it clicks.
  4. Mount behind the two spokes, screw + washer + nut per spoke, nut caps pressed on, driver side; dummy cap clipped into the left spoke.
  5. Turn the wheel lock to lock: nothing must touch the stalks.

Print files also coming on MakerWorld. Other wheels: Fusion 360 scripts with editable dimensions in hardware/fusion.

6. Boot image or GIF (optional)

Shown while the M5Dial waits for the phone, inside the spinning ring or full screen. Plug the M5Dial in over USB-C (firmware 1.1 or later, installed with the button above), pick a picture or an animated GIF, then send it.

Square crop. Animated GIF: up to 300 frames, quality and frame count lowered by itself if it does not fit.

7. Wireless updates (optional)

Web UI from the phone: on the M5Dial, Settings → Web UI, press twice, pick the hotspot, then open the address it shows in the phone's browser: every setting, page order, LEDs, hotspot, export / import of the config file, paired devices and the boot image or GIF, without a computer.

The M5Dial can update itself through your phone's hotspot, no computer needed: on the M5Dial, Settings → Update, press twice, pick your hotspot in the list and type its password with the wheel (turn = character, press = add, hold = done). It is saved for next time. Optional: you can also send the hotspot name and password from here over USB-C.

Then, when an update is out: turn the hotspot on (iPhone: Maximize Compatibility on, the M5Dial only sees 2.4 GHz), and on the M5Dial go to Settings → Update, press twice. It connects, compares versions, downloads, then restarts (Bluetooth comes back after the restart). Settings and pairing are kept.
